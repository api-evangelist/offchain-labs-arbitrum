Mastercard has announced a major expansion of its global settlement capabilities , choosing Arbitrum as one of the networks to support its new onchain infrastructure. As payment flows shift toward an internet-native paradigm, Mastercard is making 24/7 financial operations a reality by introducing intraday, weekend, and holiday settlement options.
